A two-line warning to my past self, written by a small program for the
[Two Lines to Your Past Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca)
bounty on make.fun.

Line one warns my past self about one specific moment in a single
sentence: that night in the kitchen, when she asked "Are we okay?" and I
answered "We're fine" just to end the conversation. Line two says in one
sentence why that moment mattered: she believed me and stopped asking,
so two winters later she left still waiting for the honest answer I
never gave her — one small evasion, traced to exactly what it cost.

## The entry

> That night in the kitchen, when she asks, 'Are we okay?', don't answer 'We're fine' just to end the conversation.
>
> She believed you and stopped asking, so two winters later she left still waiting for the honest answer you never gave her.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt`.
